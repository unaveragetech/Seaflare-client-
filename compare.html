Seaflare Market

Unofficial, community-made client modification for Fabric 1.21.4. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang Studios, DonutSMP, or CCBlueX. Bundles a modified build of the GPL-3.0-licensed Seaflare (LiquidBounce) client, plus Belfegor (MIT) and a Baritone runtime, both embedded unmodified — see the deep-dive page for full attribution.
